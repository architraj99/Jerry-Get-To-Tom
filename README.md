Jerry! Get to Tom

A small Tom and Jerry browser platformer....

Jerry carries medicine through from old house to reach an injured Tom before a 45 second countdown runs out...

Jump across wooden blocks andd  avoid spikes and gaps  and collect  cheese along the way.....


## what actually i added in this new update ??

## New features

- I reduced the 45 second timer to 30 second to make the game more hard
- At every 3 cheese you get 2 second extra added to your life....
- Earlier the platforms was rigid.... now it can get colapse at any time shortly after landing on them... then return after a delay.
- Bouncy cushions launch Jerry higher than a normal jump.(( ( **some are too dangerous , that's the ragebait** ))
- Moving wooden shelves carry Jerry between platforms.
- Steam traps show a warning puff before releasing dangerous steam....
- Extra upper platforms give players more ways through the level.... )
- Pause and resume using P...Escape... or the buttons on screen
- The results screen shows more detailed statistics.... cheese collected, jumps, checkpoint resets, and bonus seconds....
- Successful rescues earn Gold  Silver  or Bronze based on time, cheese collected, and resets....
- Your fastest successful rescue is saved in your browser and stays after reloading....
- Made some new art works


## The idea

This project started with a short rescue story: 
Tom is hurt.... and Jerry has to bring his medicine. 



## How to play

1. Accept the mission on the opening manuscript page....
2. Read the instructions and press PLAY
3. The timer stays at TIME: 45 sec until you do any movements 
4. Cross the house and reach Tom on the final ground section.....



A - MOVE LEFT

D - MOVE RIGHT 

W OR SPACE - JUMP
